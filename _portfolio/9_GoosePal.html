" collection: portfolio --- My project explores how augmented reality can be used to make outdoor walking feel more engaging and intentional. Rather than focusing on performance or competition, strollingPal centers on curiosity, exploration, and a light companion that follows the user through their stroll. The experience combines a virtual goose, collectible gems, and small interactions that reward looking around and discovering the environment differently. In this sense, the project aims to support motivation without turning the walk into another screen-heavy task. Built for HoloLens 2 with Unity and MRTK, the project relies on hand-based interactions. Users can pick up gems with a pinch gesture, take photos with a two-hand framing gesture, and help the goose shake off bees when it gets attacked. The goose moves across a NavMesh generated from the real-world environment through HoloLens 2 spatial mapping, allowing it to react naturally to the user's surroundings. Key Features

  • AR Walking Companion: A virtual goose accompanies the user outdoors and adds a playful presence to a regular stroll.
  • Gesture-Based Interactions: Pinching, framing, and hand-shaking gestures let the user collect gems, take photos, and interact with the companion without breaking immersion.
  • Environment-Aware Navigation: The goose relies on spatial mapping and a NavMesh to move within the real world in a believable way.
  • Curiosity-Driven Rewards: Gems and small interactions encourage exploration and make the walk feel more engaging over time.
