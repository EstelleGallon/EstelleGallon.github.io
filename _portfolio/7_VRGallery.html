--- title: "VR Gallery - A critic of dark patterns" excerpt: " This project involved the design and development of a virtual reality experience that critique dark patterns in user interfaces.
" collection: portfolio --- My project criticises how contemporary systems simulate consent rather than genuinely obtain it. In the context of generative AI, artist's work is often collected, processed and reused without meaningful consent, attribution nor compensation. Artists often don't even know their art was stolen by robots that scrape the web. This project focuses dark patterns of non-informed consent where: terms and conditions are technically accessible but unreadable (due to the language used or length of them) Participation is considered as consent Users are not aware of the consequences of their actions
All these elements create a power imbalance between the users (artists), which provide value unknowingly by being manipulated into giving their consent, and the AI system that extracts, transforms and monetizes the said value.

Key Features

  • Immersive VR Experience: I took advantage of 3D space to create a sense of immersion and presence, enhancing the physical friction that exists in terms and conditions and consent.
  • Interactive Drawing Canvas: I created a drawing canvas in VR where users can create their own art.
  • Dark Pattern Critique: The experience highlights various dark patterns related to user consent and data exploitation.
